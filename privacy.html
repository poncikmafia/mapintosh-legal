Privacy Policy

mapintosh uploader · Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Summary

The mapintosh uploader ("the app") is a desktop tool used only by the owner of the mapintosh channel to upload mapintosh's own videos to the mapintosh TikTok account. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of other people.

2. Data the app uses

  • TikTok sign-in tokens for the mapintosh account, received through Login Kit after the owner signs in. They are needed to upload videos.
  • Basic account information of the mapintosh account (such as its account ID and display name), used only to confirm which account videos are sent to.
  • The videos and captions created by mapintosh, which are sent to TikTok.

3. Where the data is kept

Tokens and account information are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. Nothing is sent anywhere other than to TikTok's own API.

4. Sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not process data of TikTok users other than the mapintosh account itself.

5. Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and by deleting the locally stored token files.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent through the mapintosh YouTube channel: youtube.com/@mapintosh.
